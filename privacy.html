Privacy Policy — Spades

Last updated: November 25, 2026

Spades is a card game for 2 to 4 players. This policy explains what information the game collects, why it collects it, and what it does not do. In short: the game collects only what it needs to let you play with other people, shows no ads, runs no analytics, sells no data, and uses no third-party tracking.

What the game collects

  • An anonymous player ID. When you first open the game, it signs you in anonymously through Google Firebase Authentication. This creates a random ID (a long string of letters and numbers) that identifies your device to the game. It is not linked to your name, email address, phone number, or any account. You never create an account or a password.
  • The player name and game handle you choose. If you set a display name or claim a game handle (for example, to be found by friends), that name is stored so other players can see it and invite you. You can change it, and you can delete your game handle at any time from inside the game. Only the person who claimed a handle can delete it.
  • Game state. Your cards, bids, scores, and the state of any multiplayer table you are sitting at are stored in Google Firebase Realtime Database while the game is running, so all players at a table see the same game. Tables that nobody has been in for 2 hours are deleted automatically.
  • A push notification token. If you allow table notifications, your device gets a push token from Firebase Cloud Messaging so the game can tell you when someone joins your table or a seat opens up. The token is stored only so notifications can reach your device. You can turn notifications off at any time from the game's menu.

What the game does not collect

  • No email address, phone number, or contacts. Invites sent by text or share use your own phone's messaging and sharing — the game never sees your address book.
  • No location data.
  • No advertising identifier, and no ads of any kind.
  • No analytics or usage tracking.
  • No data is sold to anyone, and no data is shared with advertisers or data brokers.

Who the data is shared with

Game data is stored and processed by Google Firebase (Google LLC) — the service that runs the game's database, anonymous sign-in, and push notifications. Firebase acts as a service provider for the game; it does not use your game data for its own advertising. The game is hosted by Vercel Inc., which may keep standard web server logs (such as IP addresses) as part of operating the hosting service. No other company receives your data.

How long data is kept

Multiplayer tables are deleted automatically once nobody has been in them for 2 hours. Your anonymous player ID and chosen names stay until you delete them in the game or clear the game's data on your device. Push tokens are removed when you turn notifications off.

Children

Spades is not directed at children under 13, and it does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The game asks for no personal information at all — only the anonymous ID and the names you choose to enter.

Your choices

  • Play solo without joining any multiplayer table — no game data leaves your device except the anonymous sign-in itself.
  • Change or delete your game handle from the Friends screen in the game.
  • Turn table notifications off from the game's menu.
  • Clear the game's data (or uninstall it) to remove everything stored on your device.
