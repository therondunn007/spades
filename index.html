How to play

  • Goal: be the first player (or team) to 35 points.
  • Deal: the deck is shuffled fresh every deal. 4 players get 13 cards each; 3 players get 17 (the 2♣ sits out); 2 players get 13 each and the rest of the deck sits out.
  • Look first, then bid: after each deal your cards stay on screen for as long as you need — no timer. When you're ready, tap Ready — make my bid and bidding starts.
  • Bidding: everyone bids how many tricks they'll take. In 4-player games, partners' bids and tricks combine.
  • Play: follow the suit led if you can. Highest card of the led suit wins — unless a spade is played. Spades are trump.
  • House rule: spades may be led at any time, right from the first trick. You don't have to wait for spades to be broken.
  • Scoring — pick before you start (on the start screen or in Menu → Scoring). First to 35 wins either way.
  • Bid = points: make your bid and you score exactly your bid — bid 4, score 4. Extra tricks add nothing. Miss your bid and you lose your bid.
  • Standard: make your bid and you score 2 points per trick bid + 1 per extra trick. Miss your bid and you lose 1 point per trick bid.
  • Nil (bid 0): take no tricks for +10; take any trick and it's −10. In partners, a nil is scored on its own, separate from your partner's bid.
  • The winner of each trick leads the next one. Dealer rotates every hand.
